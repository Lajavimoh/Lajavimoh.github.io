About Me

I design systems that reduce cognitive load, not just screens

I have a habit of falling down rabbit holes.

Sometimes it's because I'm trying to understand why one product feels effortless while another makes me want to close the tab. Sometimes it's because I decided rebuilding my portfolio with AI sounded more fun than updating it.

That curiosity has shaped the last 8 years of my career. I've designed products across SaaS, fintech, e-commerce and AI, but what keeps me interested has never really changed. Finding the simplest way to solve a genuinely complicated problem.

These days you'll usually find me somewhere between Figma, Claude, and a notebook full of ideas that started with, "I wonder if there's a better way to do this."
